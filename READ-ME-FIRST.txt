NEW NAVIGATION + LIGHT/DARK MODE
================================

No database change. This is the biggest change so far — it replaces the whole
menu — so upload it when you have a few minutes to click around afterwards.

It ALSO carries the full-screen viewer and editable descriptions from the
previous zip, which had not gone up yet. You do not need that one.

HOW TO UPLOAD (GitHub web)
--------------------------
1. Open https://github.com/UofTNeuromuscularFellowship/Fellowship
2. Add file -> Upload files
3. Extract this zip and DRAG THESE THREE ONTO THE PAGE:

       src                 (folder)
       index.html          (file)
       tailwind.config.js  (file)

   Do not drag the folder that contains them, and do not drag this readme.
4. Commit. Vercel redeploys on its own.

WHAT CHANGES
------------
DESKTOP. A rail of seven icons down the left: Home, Clinic, Teaching, Case Log,
EMG Toolkit, Program, Settings. Click one and a panel opens beside it listing
what is in that area, with the area's overview in the main pane — a card per
tool saying what it is for. The panel collapses with the arrow at its top, and
it remembers whether you left it open.

PHONE. Tap Menu and you get the same seven as tiles. Tap one for its tools, tap
a tool to open it. "All areas" goes back up one level. An area with only one
thing in it (Clinic, Home) opens that thing directly.

LIGHT / DARK. Three choices — Light, Auto, Dark. Auto follows whatever the
device is set to and is the default. It is remembered per device, so your ward
computer and your phone can differ. The switch is at the bottom of the rail, in
the phone menu, and in Settings under "Appearance".
