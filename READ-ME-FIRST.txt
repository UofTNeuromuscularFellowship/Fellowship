WHAT CHANGES
------------
- A stacked icon rail down the left: All, Waveform, Ultrasound, MRI, Muscle
  biopsy, Examination finding, each with a count. Same style as the portal's
  main rail. On a phone it is a scrolling strip across the top.
- The cases now fill the pane that used to say "Choose a case from the list" —
  a card each, with a thumbnail, kind, what it shows, annotation count, and
  who uploaded it.
- Clicking a card opens the case as before; "All cases" goes back to the grid.
- The open case now also names the uploader in its header.

ABOUT THE UPLOADER NAMES
------------------------
This needed a small database function, already applied. Your user table is
locked down so that a fellow can only read their own record — sensible, but it
meant a fellow would have seen their own name and blanks for everyone else. The
new function returns ONLY the id and full name, and only for people who have
actually uploaded a case. No email, no role, no status, and nothing at all
about members who have never uploaded. Your own uploads read "uploaded by you".
