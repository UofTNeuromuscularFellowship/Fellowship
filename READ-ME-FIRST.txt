WHAT CHANGES
------------
- A stacked icon rail down the left, same style as the image library and the
  main portal menu: All tools, then the four calculators, then the two
  teaching tools, under their own small headings.
- The tool you pick fills the pane beside it. Moving between tools is now one
  click instead of going back to the grid and in again.
- "All tools" returns to the card grid, which is unchanged — the descriptions
  there are how someone meets a tool they have not used before.
- The text badges (TLI, F/H, SR, DM) are now drawn icons matching the rest of
  the app.
- The "Educational use only" notice stays above everything, in both states.

Nothing about the calculations, cutoffs or interpretations has changed.
