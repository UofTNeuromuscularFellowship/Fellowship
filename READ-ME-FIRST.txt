WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
Not the position — the column. The icon rail stretched to the height of the
whole PAGE rather than the window, so "bottom of the rail" meant the bottom of
the document. On the dashboard that put the appearance control about 500 pixels
below the fold. On a short page it happened to be visible, which is why it came
and went.

WHAT CHANGES
------------
- The rail and the side panel are now pinned to the window. Their foot is
  always the foot of the screen, whatever the page length, and the menu stays
  put while you scroll.
- Your name, role, the Light/Auto/Dark switch and Sign out are now behind ONE
  button: a circle with your initials, bottom-left of the rail on a computer
  and top-right of the header on a phone. They used to be split across two
  different columns.
- The phone's Menu overlay no longer repeats them — the account button in the
  header is one tap away, and two appearance switches would not have stayed in
  step with each other.

Nothing else moved. Click the circle to see everything about your account.
